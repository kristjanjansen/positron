# The film that was behind `/stage/`, 2026-09-18 to 2026-09-19

`Le Voyage dans la Lune`, Melies, 1902. It was the stage picture for one day
and it was replaced on instruction: *"rm going to the moon video in stage,
video win to 16:9 and replace with mim sustsinable kirikutseen"*. What is
there now is the church scene from `MIM goes Sustainable`, a recording
MIMproject made and holds, taken from `demo/resources/mimproject.json` by its
`id`.

**The record is here and the file is not.** `moon-1902.json` carries the
source item, the in and out points of the excerpt, and the encode line, so
the 4.2 MB `moon-1902.mp4` can be made again from the source rather than
being kept as a derivative nothing uses. It was deleted from
`demo/resources/`, where it was also being copied into the deploy on every
build.

**Two decisions in it are worth keeping and neither is about this film.** A
public domain claim on two independent grounds beats a better looking one on
one: `Hexentanz` and `Metropolis` were both rejected for having one. And the
audio question is decided by what the artefact IS, not by size: the 1902 film
is silent, so any sound on an upload of it is a modern score with its own
copyright, and it was dropped. The Dickson Experimental Sound Film before it
went the other way, because there the 1894 sound was the artefact.
