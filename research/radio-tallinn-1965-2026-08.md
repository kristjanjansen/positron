# Radio Tallinn 1965 — the first named client (2026-08-27)

Working theses received from the same PhD circle as the Kurenniemi work
(plan-timeline §−1): a contemporary-music platform on the counterfactual of an
electronic-music studio Tallinn could have had in 1965. ERR archives 1965–1991
as "unfinished compositions"; programmes pair archival recordings with living
responses; editorial programming over recommendation; fixed slots (28/56/112
min); scheduled silence; live as highest form; subscription-only; no listener
data beyond aggregate.

**Status: the "real show asking by name" that C8/C9 wait for.** The §−1 ERR
horizon made concrete, with a format spec. Candidate v1 driver alongside
grid-archive replay.

## Mapping to existing machinery (~1:1)

- Schedule of fixed slots = pre-authored timeline of spans (score system).
- "Live broadcast as highest form" = `live = playhead at now`, verbatim.
- Scheduled silence = plan-studio's "silence is a valid config", already baked.
- ERR tape ingest = §−1 archival adapters (spans w/ internal time, provenance).
- Thesis 30 (source recording unedited beside the work made from it) = the
  demo10/tratteggio evidence-vs-derived overlay elevated from inspection UI to
  programming format — confirms §5b's bet that the attested/dreamed boundary is
  performance material.

## Design pressure it adds

- **Thesis 17 is a tier-0 evidence policy stricter than §5b imagined**: no
  noise reduction, no normalization, no mastering on archival material ("the
  temporal signature; removing it is historical falsification"). Validates the
  FORCED evidence-policy choice (no silent default) and implies:
  mastering/normalization is itself a declared DERIVED lane, never in-place.
- Noise-as-signature ⇒ capture-chain metadata (equipment, tape speed, transfer
  parameters) is provenance — the PREMIS bridge earns its place.
- Thesis 29: liveness must be surfaced honestly in the player (live vs replay
  labeling) — one-render-path with truthful state.
- Gaps/silence are scheduled content, not errors (window() returning nothing
  is meaningful; independently confirms §5b "perform the lacunae").
- Listener contract (no data, no feed) vs the measure-everything culture:
  resolvable — measure the rig, never the listener — but each beacon must be
  consciously placed on one side of that line.
- The founding counterfactual ("the studio that never existed") is tier-3
  generative restoration at the level of history itself; "trajectory, not
  nostalgia" restates the fabrication-made-honest doctrine. Thesis 11 ("the
  archive is an instrument") = Ernst's dynarchive — already named in
  timeline-art-prior-art.md as the thesis of the whole plan. Two independent
  derivations of the same doctrines.
