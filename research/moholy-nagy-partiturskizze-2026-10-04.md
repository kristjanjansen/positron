## 1. What the object is

**Title, as printed on the plate (SEEN, photos and scan):** *L. Moholy-Nagy:
Partiturskizze zu einer mechanischen Exzentrik. Synthese von Form, Bewegung,
Ton, Licht (Farbe) und Geruch.*

**Where it was published (READ).** *Die Bühne im Bauhaus*, Bauhausbücher 4,
Albert Langen Verlag, München, series editors Walter Gropius and L.
Moholy-Nagy, typography by Moholy-Nagy, title page by Schlemmer, printed by
Dietsch & Brückner, Weimar. Contents: Schlemmer *Mensch und Kunstfigur* (7 to
24), Schlemmer illustrations (26 to 43), **Moholy-Nagy *Partiturskizze* (44)**,
Moholy-Nagy *Theater, Zirkus, Varieté* (45 onward), Molnár *U-Theater* (57 to
62), Moholy-Nagy illustrations (63 to 65), Breuer, Kurt Schmidt with F. W.
Bogler and Georg Teltscher, Schawinsky, the Bauhauskapelle.
Source: [archive.org scan](https://archive.org/details/schlemmer-et-al.-1925-die-buhne-am-bauhaus),
its [OCR text](https://archive.org/download/schlemmer-et-al.-1925-die-buhne-am-bauhaus/Schlemmer%20et%20al.%20-%201925%20-%20Die%20B%C3%BChne%20am%20Bauhaus_djvu.txt).

**The date, settled better than the brief had it (READ).** The book's own
imprint page says: *"Dieses Buch wurde im Frühjahr 1924 zusammengestellt.
Technische Schwierigkeiten verhinderten das rechtzeitige Erscheinen."* and that
the Bauhaus had meanwhile closed in Weimar and continued as *"Das Bauhaus in
Dessau"*. So **the score existed by spring 1924 and was published in 1925**.
The usual dating "1924" (the drawing) or "1924/25" and "1925" (the book) are
both right about different things. The [Moholy-Nagy Foundation
chronology](https://www.moholy-nagy.org/chronology/) (READ) lists the book under
1925 and puts the writing of *Theater, Zirkus, Varieté* in **1923**; [Sigman
2018](https://data.jssa.info/paper/2018v10n01/3.Sigman.pdf) (READ) calls it
"his 1924 essay". Not settled which; both fit "compiled spring 1924".

**The physical form (READ + SEEN).** Page 44 is a caption that begins
*"Nebenstehend die PARTITUR-SKIZZE..."*, so the plate faces it as a fold-out.
In the archive.org scan the plate is photographed flat and sideways, about
2.6 times as long as it is tall, with its title rotated 90 degrees: it is
printed to be read **vertically, title at the top**, which is how the museum
hangs it. Colour lithographic printing in black, yellow, red, blue and grey is
SEEN; the medium is called a "colour lithograph" by [ARCH+](https://archplus.net/de/news/18562-sensefactory-you-have-never-experienced)
(SECONDHAND on technique; the [Letterform Archive](https://exhibitions.letterformarchive.org/bauhaus/artworks/the-theater-of-the-bauhaus-die-bu-hne-im-bauhaus-bauhaus-book-4)
gives the book as letterpress, 185 x 235 mm, and says nothing about the plate).

**What the museum most likely shows (INFERRED).** A copy of the book with the
plate unfolded and hung vertically in its accordion folds: photo 2 shows the
bound book block behind the top fold. Which edition cannot be told from the

The museum label would settle it.

## 2. How the score is meant to be read: Moholy-Nagy's own key (READ)

The caption on page 44 is the key, and almost no secondary source quotes it in
full. Paraphrased closely, sentence by sentence:

1. It is a score sketch **"für ein Varieté"**, for a variety show.
2. **The stage has three parts.** I. Bühne, the lower one, for larger forms and
   movements. II. Bühne, above, with a glass plate that folds open, for smaller
   forms and movements; *"Die Glasplatte ist zugleich präparierte
   PROJEKTIONSWAND für von der Rückseite der Bühne projizierte
   Filmvorführungen"*, the glass is also a projection screen for film
   back-projected from behind. III. (Zwischen-)Bühne, the in-between stage,
   holds **mechanical music apparatus**, mostly without sound boxes, only horns:
   percussion, noise and wind instruments. Some walls are doubled with white
   canvas that lets coloured light from spotlights and *Lichtbäume* through and
   diffuses it.
3. **Columns 1 and 2 are form and movement events "in senkrecht abwärtsgehender
   Kontinuität"**, in vertically downward continuity. Time runs down.
4. **Column 3 is light, one effect after another, and "Die Breite der Streifen
   bedeutet die Dauer"**: the width of a stripe is its duration. **"Schwarz =
   Finsternis"**, black is darkness. Narrow vertical stripes inside a wide
   stripe are **simultaneous partial lightings** of the stage.
5. **Column 4 is for music, "hier nur in den Absichten angedeutet"**, only
   indicated as intentions here. The **coloured vertical stripes are sirens of
   different kinds of howl**, accompanying a large part of the action.
6. **"Die Gleichzeitigkeit ist in der Partitur aus der Horizontale zu lesen"**:
   simultaneity is read along the horizontal.

Then a running order headed **FOLGE** (READ, from the page image, not only the
OCR, which scrambles the columns):

Arrows plunge, slats open, circles rotate, electrical apparatus, lightning and
thunder, grids of colours shooting up-down, to-and-fro, phosphorescence, giant
apparatus swinging, flashing, grids continuing, wheels, explosions, **smells**,
clowning, human mechanics. Column 2: the same opening, then **cinema on the
daylight wall, run backwards**, action, tempo wild. Columns 3 and 4 are "clear
without keywords".

Three things follow, and they matter for everything below:

- **Smell has no lane.** The title promises *Geruch*; the plate has four
  columns; smell appears once, as the fourteenth item of column 1's running
  order (READ + SEEN). Molnár's *U-Theater* in the same book lists
  *"Duftverbreiter"*, scent dispensers, among its stage machinery (READ). So
  smell is a cue inside the motion lane, not a medium with its own time axis.
- **Only one lane has a stated time scale.** Light: stripe length is duration.
  Columns 1 and 2 are "continuity" with no scale; column 4 is declared
  placeholder. **No unit, no tempo and no total length are given anywhere**
  (READ: absent from the caption and the essay). INFERRED: the light lane is
  the de facto clock and the others are aligned to it by eye.
- **The author marked his own music lane as not real.** *"Hier nur in den
  Absichten angedeutet."* The staff fragments in column 4 (SEEN) are therefore
  stand-ins for music to be written, while the coloured siren bands are the
  part he meant. This is an epistemic tier, declared by the author, in 1924.

What the plate shows (SEEN, photos and scan; identifications INFERRED where
noted): column 1 has arrows, a rotating circle, a starburst with a clock-like
hand, red and blue crossing bars over a fine grid, two black blade shapes
(INFERRED: the "Riesen-Apparate schwingen"), three eye-like target discs in red,
blue and yellow, and at the bottom a tumbling figure in a circle (INFERRED:
"Clownerie" or "Menschmechanik"). Column 2 is sparse: arrows, a half circle, a
red arrow, and a grey cone opening downward from a small circle (INFERRED: a
projector beam, "Kino"). Column 3 is the dense colour column: black, yellow,
red, blue, grey and a textured grey-white band like flashes (INFERRED:
"Phosphoreszenz" or "Blitz"), with narrow vertical stripes inside wide bands.
Column 4 has a long blue band, then yellow, red, grey bands, with staff
fragments laid across.

## 3. The essay, and a correction to how it is usually summarised (READ)

*Theater, Zirkus, Varieté* runs: 1. the historical theatre, whose elements were
already *"TON, FARBE (LICHT), BEWEGUNG, RAUM, FORM (GEGENSTÄNDE UND
MENSCHEN)"*; 2. present-day attempts: (a) the Futurist, Dada and Merz "theatre
of surprises", (b) **die mechanische Exzentrik**; 3. **the coming theatre,
Theater der Totalität**; then how to realise it, and the means.

**The Mechanical Eccentric (2b).** Described as *"eine Aktionskonzentration der
Bühne in Reinkultur"*, the stage's concentration of action in pure form, in
which the human being *"keinen Platz mehr"* has, because a body can at most
organise movement relative to its own mechanism, and its effect on the viewer is
subjective astonishment. The demand is for *"einer bis ins Letzte
beherrschbaren, exakten Form- und Bewegungsorganisation, welche die Synthese
der dynamisch kontrastierenden Erscheinungen (von Raum, Form, Bewegung, Ton und
Licht) sein sollte. Mechanische Exzentrik. (Siehe Seite 44)"*. The cross
reference is to the plate.

**Then he rejects it as the goal.** In section 3 he names *"der Fehler aller auf
dieser Basis gebauten Folgerungen (z. B. NUR mechanische Exzentrik)"*. Once the
dominance of the literary is broken, the human being *"in seiner Totalität"*
may no longer be excluded, but is used *"NEBEN DEN ANDEREN
GESTALTUNGSMITTELN GLEICHWERTIG"*, equal to the other means. The Theater der
Totalität is *"Licht, Raum, Fläche, Form, Bewegung, Ton, Mensch"* as one
organism.

So: **the Partiturskizze is the score of the step he argues past, not of the
totality theatre itself.** Many secondary summaries (for example
[acousmata](https://acousmata.com/post/1433857604/mechanized-eccentric), quoting
the 1961 translation's "humanless environmental field of lights, sounds, films,
odors, music, mechanized apparatus, and simulated explosions") present the
score as the vision of total theatre. Moholy's own text frames it as the
mechanical half, a variety number, which the total theatre then puts the
performer back into. INFERRED, and worth holding on to: the "Menschmechanik"
and the tumbling figure at the bottom of the plate are where the human comes
back in, as one more mechanism.

Lines in the essay that land directly on positron (READ):

- **Sound from unexpected places**: *"eine sprechende oder singende
  Bogenlampe, unter den Sitzplätzen oder unter dem Theaterboden ertönende
  Lautsprecher"*.
- **Light as an equal medium**: reflected projections as *"Flächenfilme und
  Raumlichtspiele"*, *"die AKTION DES LICHTES"*, light *"neben allen anderen"*
  made equal by present technique; light as sudden glare, flare-up,
  phosphorescence, flooding the auditorium while the stage goes dark.
- **Mediated simultaneity**: *"SIMULTANE, SYNOPTISCHE, SYNAKUSTISCHE
  (optisch- oder phonetisch-mechanische) Wiedergabe von Gedanken (Kino,
  Grammophon, Lautsprecher)"*, and faces enlarged by mirrors with voices
  amplified to match.
- **The audience joins in**: the masses should *"zugreifen, mittun"*, and
  keeping that from chaos is the job of *"des tausendäugigen, mit allen
  modernen Verständigungs- und Verbindungsmitteln ausgerüsteten NEUEN
  SPIELLEITERS"*, the thousand-eyed new director equipped with every modern
  means of communication and connection.
- **The stage as moving surfaces**: hanging bridges, a rotating stage,
  platforms moving front to back and up and down to pick out moments *"wie die
  Großaufnahme des Films"*.

Related writing by Moholy-Nagy (SECONDHAND,
[Fotomanifeste](http://www.fotomanifeste.de/manifeste/1922-moholy-produktion-reproduktion)
and search summaries): *Produktion, Reproduktion*, De Stijl 5:7, July 1922,
proposing that reproduction media (gramophone, photo, film) be used to
produce; *Neue Gestaltung in der Musik. Möglichkeiten des Grammophons*, Der
Sturm 14:7, July 1923, on inscribing sound directly into the record groove.
The film sketch *Dynamik der Großstadt* (1921/22) is the other "score" of the
period (SECONDHAND, below).

## 4. Was it performed?

**Not in Moholy-Nagy's lifetime, as far as any source says.** No source found
claims a performance before 1987, and two later makers call it unrealised:
Theater der Klänge (*"a theoretically utopian theatre project that remained
unrealised until Theater der Klänge staged it in 1987"*, READ,
[theaterderklaenge.de](https://theaterderklaenge.de/en/project/the-mechanical-bauhaus-stage/))
and Schroeter & Berger (*"in dieser Form noch nie realisierten Plan"*, READ,
[schroeterundberger.de](http://schroeterundberger.de/mechanische-exzentrik/)).
Sigman notes Moholy *"was not able to fully realize the aims of the theater of
totality in his lifetime"* and that Gropius's Totaltheater for Piscator was
abandoned (READ). ⚠️ Absence of a performance is an absence; I found no
evidence of one, which is weaker than evidence of none.

The parts that matter here:

- **Spans, not only instants** (plan-timeline C1), and **continuous kinds** with

- **A score is a value** (`score.mjs`), and **the envelope is normalised, never
  the payload** (plan-score §1). `unit` is mandatory even for a language with
  no tempo (plan-score §2).
- **The timeline is a trace, never an authoring format** (plan-timeline C10). A
  score is a program that quotes traces.

  line 172 to 202).
- **Reverse playback** exists in `/loops/` (the direction button) and `/typist/`.
- **The routing media** (bay.mjs, today): `midi, audio, clock, value, video,
  program, file, state`. **There is no light medium**, and the word "light" in
  plan-universal-routing §4 means light-weight media, not lighting. Lighting
  appears in plan-timeline only as **MIDI Show Control as an actuator for
  lighting desks** and as the analogy of desk *tracking* (ETC Eos's *Assert*)

- **Screens and walls exist as targets**: `/weight/` puts a sentence on four
  walls, `/floor/` lays newsreels on a floor, `/mirror/` draws a shader on a
  Pi's screen, `/click/` has lamps that players watch.

## 7. Assessment

### 7.1 What positron already does that the score does

| the score | positron | how close |
| --- | --- | --- |
| parallel lanes, one per medium | the strip's lanes; `/lanes/` and `/held/` | same idea, rotated: his time runs down, ours runs right |
| *"Gleichzeitigkeit ... aus der Horizontale"* | one playhead crossing every lane | identical. His "temporal lines that exist conceptually" (Wingham) are our playhead |
| light stripes whose length is duration | spans with `at` and `dur` (C1) | identical in kind |
| narrow stripes inside a wide one = simultaneous partial lighting | overlapping spans, or a sub-lane | representable, never drawn this way yet |
| black = darkness | `/radio1965` theses: silence is scheduled content, not an error | same stance: an empty lane is a value |
| "Kino auf Tageswand, rückwärts gedreht" | reverse playback in `/loops/` | a cue we can already perform |
| the music lane declared "nur in den Absichten" | evidence tiers; derived lanes; the tratteggio view | his tier is declared by the author on the plate itself; ours is declared in data |
| a running order (FOLGE) beside a graphic score | `/held/` reads a running order and draws it; `/score/` lights the line playing | the FOLGE list is literally a cue list for columns 1 and 2 |

### 7.2 What it does that positron does not

1. **The lanes are bound to places.** The axonometric at the top is part of the
   score: I. Bühne, II. Bühne with its glass Projektionswand, III. Bühne for the
   machines. Column headings name their stage ("Form + Bewegung (I. Bühne)",
   "+ Kino (II. Bühne)"). Positron's lanes are named by `kind`; where a lane
   *goes* is the routing graph's job, and no score carries its own map of
   targets. INFERRED: this is the most interesting gap, because the routing
   work is happening today.
2. **Light and colour as a lane in its own right**, with duration encoded as
   length. `/held/` has a tint lane, but it is data read from someone else's
   score, not an authored light lane driving anything.
3. **A projection surface as a target**, distinct from "a screen": the same
   glass is a stage for small objects AND a back-projection screen. Positron has
   `screen` nodes; it has no notion of one surface carrying two media.
4. **Form and motion as notation**: arrows, rotation, grids, swinging blades.
   Vectors and rotations, not events. Positron's `value` and continuous kinds
   could carry them (`/draw/` records a path); nothing draws them as glyphs.
5. **Smell**, as a single cue. No actuator, no plan, no reason to want one.
6. **No declared time unit.** Not a feature: it is what makes the plate
   unperformable as written, and what every realisation had to invent.

### 7.3 What positron does that the score cannot

Seek, rate and pause; a score you can run backwards from any point; a measured
fire time per event; quotation with marks so a re-cut source survives; JSON
round-trip; several sites. INFERRED: the plate is the classic case of
plan-timeline C10's boundary. It is pure authoring, and **no trace of a
performance by its author exists**. Every staging since (1987, 2009, 2019) is a
program quoting it.

### 7.4 The heritage reading (INFERRED, flagged as such)

The plate is a near-perfect specimen for the archival client the heritage memory
describes, and better than most because it is small, public domain in the
source scan (archive.org marks it with the Public Domain Mark; Moholy-Nagy died
1946, which puts the work out of copyright in the EU since 2017 on the life plus
70 rule, ⚠️ a legal reading, not advice) and fully documented by its own author.

- **One attested source, several derived works.** The plate is tier 0. Theater
  der Klänge 1987 *"newly created ... within the aesthetic framework"*, Schroeter
  & Berger 2009, SENSEFACTORY 2019: three derived works, three different
  readings of the same lanes. That is the tratteggio view (source beside work)
  with real data, and it is the Radio Tallinn 1965 strand that *"presents the
  SOURCE recording unedited beside the work made from it"*, transposed from
  sound to a score.
- **"Unfinished compositions."** The Radio Tallinn theses treat archive material
  as unfinished compositions. Moholy's caption says of his own music lane that
  it is only intentions. The archive does not get more literally unfinished
  than that.
- **Kurenniemi's DIMI-O (1971)** read a camera image, thresholded to black and
  white, as a 32-step score (`research/ojanen-ekis-notes.md`, p. 166 of
  Ojanen). A picture read as a score, by machine, is the step from Moholy's
  plate to Kurenniemi's instrument. That is a lineage line worth one sentence in
  any heritage write-up, not a claim of influence (none is known to me).
- **Hirschfeld-Mack rewrote his light scores from memory in 1963.** A
  reconstruction from notes and memory, by the author, forty years on, is
  exactly the "restored at a declared tier" case. It belongs in
  `research/timeline-art-prior-art.md` beside Decasia and the Disintegration
  Loops.

## 9. What I could not settle

- **Which copy the museum shows**: 1925 original, 1965 Kupferberg facsimile, or
  the Lars Müller facsimile. The label or the paper would say.
- **Whether the plate is lithography** or another process. One secondary source
  says colour lithograph; the Letterform Archive says the book is letterpress
  and is silent on the plate.
- **When *Theater, Zirkus, Varieté* was written**: 1923 (Foundation
  chronology) or 1924 (Sigman). The book was compiled spring 1924, published
  1925; that much is READ.
- **Whether anything was staged before 1987.** I found no claim of it; two
  makers call it unrealised. Absence, not proof.
- **The Triadisches Ballett music at the 1922 premiere** (Wikipedia says
  Hindemith; my memory says Hindemith is 1926). Not needed for this document.
- **How Theater der Klänge timed it in 1987.** Their notes on the time scale
  they chose would be the most useful single thing for suggestion 3, and I did
  not find them. Their Zenodo record
  ([Historical Bauhaus Stage Productions Actualized for Our Time](https://zenodo.org/records/4084788))
  was not opened and is the place to look.
- **The 1961 English text** of the essay and caption (the Monoskop PDF answered
  403). Everything quoted here is from the German original, which is better
  anyway.
- **What the glyphs in columns 1 and 2 are.** Matching FOLGE items to drawn
  shapes is my inference in every case except where the shape is the word
  (arrows, circles).
