Kujundus pildist

Every event picture below is read as this page opens, twice. At 64 pixels for its colour: a light picture gives a light scheme with a ground taken from a flat edge, or a pastel of its hue, or a light grey when it has no hue; a dark picture inverts the whole page, light text on a dark ground of its hue or a dark grey. An accent for underlines, focus and the filled button is toned from the picture's strongest hue until it reads on that ground. At up to 600 pixels for its type: text-like shapes are measured for stroke contrast and for feet at the baseline, which is what separates a Garamond from a grotesque, and the verdict is serif, sans, unsure, or no type found.

Beside each picture a whole event page is drawn in what it gave: the head, the two buttons in the system face in both modes, a longer body, the credits, a rich text block and a mailing block. When the graphic's type reads as a serif, the title, the facts and the body take one; in light text on a dark ground the body stays in the sans and only the title and facts take the serif.
