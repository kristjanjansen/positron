Elektron · surfaces

Four things to look at and one to play. Phone-ready; sound is off until you tap — iOS insists.

  1. 01megatimeline→

    The whole ERR archive as one zoomable century — 1908 to 2026, pinch to dive into a year.

    census shipped · offline until you dive
  2. 02remixer→

    Stack archive recordings from any year on one playhead and play them together.

    live search · one shared transport
  3. 03kurenniemi→

    Erkki Kurenniemi's corpus — the electronic-music instrument builder who recorded his own life.

    corpus shipped · media from archive.org
  4. 04flipper→

    Every live ERR radio and TV channel, flipped between like a car radio.

    live streams · needs a tap to start
  5. 05looper→

    An instrument, not a viewer. Play the keys, tap REC once to start recording and again to set the loop — the length is whatever you just played. Every tap after that layers another part on top.

    nothing to install · works offline once loaded
Prototypes, not products — rough edges are the point. Archive material stays on ERR's and archive.org's servers; nothing is re-hosted here. The looper talks to no server at all once the page has loaded. Search traffic to arhiiv.err.ee runs through one global gate at ≤1 request per second with a daily ceiling — what it has actually done.
