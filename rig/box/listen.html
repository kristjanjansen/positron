listen to the box

The instrument is a process somewhere else — a container here, a Raspberry Pi later. It makes the sound and sends raw samples over a socket; this page only plays what arrives and sends key presses back. Nothing is synthesised here. Play with the a w s e d f t g y h u j k row, or click the keys; z and x shift the octave.
