play it — the Pro is the instrument

Press a s d f g h j k or click. The key press goes straight to the Pro over a direct link; the sound is made there and comes back as live audio. Most of the delay you hear is the cushion the browser keeps so that audio arriving unevenly still plays smoothly — measured at 98.6% of the round trip, which is why a machine in the same room sounds much like one across the country. The cushion is reported below; it cannot be switched off.
