strip.mjs — ambiguation, the three answers of necessary, and deep time

Everything below is drawn by the shipped component from deck queries. No row is pushed into a lane. The Kurenniemi panel is the real 22-row corpus, whose innerFrom/innerTo are null on every row — so the "saturated certain core" of two-tone ambiguation is empty for the whole archive, and the panel exists to prove that an empty core is drawn as a statement rather than as an absence.

1 — the real corpus (22 rows, 22/22 smeared, 0/22 with a core)

Skirt = the possible flanks. The error bar is the empty core: bounds known, extent within them unrecorded — Gschwandtner et al.'s other recommended encoding for the same task, used because ambiguation degenerates to one tone when the core is empty. Hard terminators + the small caret = kind:'ignorance' (a real day the catalogue lost; narrowing it is a repair). The stepped silhouette is the aoristic sum with its method printed on the canvas.

2 — the three states side by side (synthetic, so all three can exist at once)

Same bracket in every lane. core: an ordered inner pair, so inner containment answers exactly — the one state where a saturated rectangle is a true statement. outer: no inner pair, bracket inside the window → sound but incomplete. unanswerable: no inner pair and the bracket is not contained → undecidable, ghosted rather than deleted. crisp: no when at all, a note-on/note-off pair.

3 — when.kind: the edge is the claim

Identical brackets, identical positions, one flag apart. ignorance — there IS a boundary and the catalogue lost it, so the bound is a fact: hard terminator, narrowing caret. vagueness — there is no boundary, so a hard edge would be the falsification: feathered, and no affordance at all. Never a dash for either; dash is tratteggio's ink, one axis down.

4 — deep time: 13.8 Gyr on the same axis as a MIDI phrase

Same component, same absolute-ms position domain, same tick LOD. Before the ladder reached past 100 y this view chose major = 100 y at 1.0e-5 px and the axis loop ran 138,000,000 times a frame. The regime swap is ChronoZoom's: the span selects the tick source, it never warps the axis.
