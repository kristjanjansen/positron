THE TYPEWRITER ON /typist/typewriter/ IS A HERMES PRECISA 305
================================================================

The nine files the page plays live in R2, bucket `positron-station`, under
`typewriter/hermes-precisa-305/`, served by the station worker at
https://positron-station.kristjan-jansen.workers.dev/media/typewriter/hermes-precisa-305/<file>
(asked 2026-10-11: *"lets move samples to r2"*). A copy of this file sits beside
them as `SOURCES.md`. The mp3s are not in this repository.

They are cut from recordings by Joseph Sardin,
published by BigSoundBank and dedicated to the public domain.

WHAT WAS DONE TO THEM
=====================

    trimmed   to start at the strike, so a key sounds the moment it is pressed
              (the published key file opens on 210 ms of silence, the space
              bar on 255 ms)
    faded     in over 2 ms and out over the last 40 ms, so a cut does not click
    encoded   ffmpeg libmp3lame, 48 kHz mono, 128 kbps

key-2 to key-5 are single strikes picked out of "Typewriter #6", a 7.2 s take
of slow typing, by their loudness envelope: the loudest onsets with at least
340 ms of quiet after them.

return.mp3 is UNCONFIRMED BY EAR. It was picked out of "Typewriter #7", a 43 s
take, by its envelope alone: 160 ms of sustained sliding sound, a thunk, a
400 ms decay and then 0.7 s of quiet, which no strike in the takes looks like.
Nothing here was listened to when it was cut. If it is not a carriage return,
knufds' "Typewriter bell and carriage reset" on Freesound (CC0) is the
replacement named in research/typewriter-samples-2026-10-11.md.
