Host self-test

Your synth, your interface, no partner. Answers one question: what will the player on the other end actually feel when they press a key?

1 · Setup

MIDI goes out to the instrument's MIDI IN; its audio comes back through your interface. Pick both.

2 · Monitoring — the setting that makes this honest

Direct monitoring lies to you. Your fingers land on a synth you hear instantly, while the player hears it a full round-trip later. With return-path monitoring on, you are sitting in the player's chair: every millisecond of the tests below is a millisecond you feel. Turn it off only to A/B the two, and turn it straight back on.
